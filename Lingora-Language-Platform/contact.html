Common Questions

Before you reach out

What's the fastest way to get support?

Email is fastest for account and billing issues; use the contact form above for anything else.

Do you offer institutional partnerships?

Yes — select "Partnership" in the form above and our team will follow up with details.

How do I reset my password?

Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password" — we'll email you a secure reset link within minutes.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

Yes, all paid plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee — just reach out through the contact form above.

How long does it take to hear back?

Our team typically replies within 24 hours, and often much sooner for urgent billing or account issues.
