Our Story

Started by learners, built for learners

Lingora began in 2019 when a small team of linguists and engineers set out to fix a simple problem: most people quit language apps within weeks. We rebuilt learning around daily habits, real speaking practice, and genuine motivation.

Today, over 4 million learners across 190+ countries use Lingora to build real fluency — not just app streaks.

Our Mission

Make real fluency achievable for anyone, anywhere, regardless of budget or background, through effective, enjoyable daily practice.

Our Vision

A world where language is never a barrier to opportunity, connection, or curiosity.
